Privacy Policy

Tamago Math: Times Tables (a math practice app for elementary school children)

Summary

  • We do not collect any information about your child.
  • Records and names are saved only on your device and are never sent anywhere.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The screens your child uses have no links to other websites and no way to buy anything.
  • The free trial and the monthly subscription are only on the parent screen. Payments are handled by Apple, and the developer never receives your payment information.

1. About this policy

This page explains how “Tamago Math: Times Tables” (shown as “Tamago Math” on the Home Screen; the same app is called “たまごと けいさん れんしゅう” in Japanese) handles information about children and parents.

The app is made for elementary school children. We hope parents will read this page so their children can use the app with peace of mind.

2. Information we collect

The developer does not collect any information about children or parents.

The app has no way to send information to the developer's servers or to any other company (third party). There are no accounts and no sign-in.

3. Information saved on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device. It is never sent off the device, and the developer cannot see it.

  • Your child's name (only if entered; the app works without it)
  • Practice records (right or wrong answers, time taken, dates and so on), the character's growth, badges and the calendar
  • Settings (sound effects, read aloud, text size, reading voice, language)
  • The parent passcode (stored in a form that cannot be turned back into the number)
  • Whether Face ID or Touch ID is used to open the parent screen
  • Trial and subscription status (the trial start date and whether you are subscribed), the monthly price shown by the App Store, and the App Store country. These come from the App Store and are used to show the right plan, price and notices.

4. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app shows no ads. It does not use analytics tools, usage measurement or tracking (following you across other apps or websites).

5. Read aloud, Face ID and other device features

  • Read aloud: the app only uses voices that run on your device. Problems and names are never sent off the device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (off by default): used only when a parent turns it on, to confirm a parent before opening the parent screen. Face and fingerprint data are managed by your device (iOS); the app never receives or stores them.

6. In-app purchases

  • The app offers a free 30-day trial and an auto-renewing monthly subscription. Both can only be started from the parent screen (behind the parent passcode).
  • Purchases, payments and cancellations go through Apple's App Store (your Apple ID). Your name, Apple ID, card details and other payment information are handled by Apple and are never sent to the developer. The developer does not use its own servers or any outside payment service.
  • To decide whether practice is available, the app receives only the trial start date and subscription status from the App Store, on your device, and saves them on your device. This information is never sent to the developer.
  • The Terms of Use are Apple's standard End User License Agreement (EULA). You can open them from “Terms of Use” on the parent screen (the app asks you to confirm before opening Apple's web page in your browser).

7. Backups

A file containing the records is created only when a parent taps “Save records” on the parent screen. The parent chooses where to save it (for example, the Files app or iCloud Drive). The app never sends the file anywhere by itself.

8. Deleting records

Deleting the app from your device deletes all records, names and settings saved on the device. If you made backup files, please delete them from where you saved them too.

Trial and subscription status is recorded with your Apple ID, so it is not removed when you delete the app (it carries over if you reinstall with the same Apple ID). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. To cancel, go to iPhone/iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

9. Children's privacy

The app is designed so that no personal information is collected from children. We built it with children's privacy laws and guidance in mind, including the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the Australian Privacy Act and Canada's PIPEDA. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing to share, sell or use for advertising.

  • Entering a name is optional, and the name never leaves the device.
  • Parent features such as changing the name, the learning report, backups, starting or managing the trial and subscription, and opening the Terms of Use are behind the parent passcode (or Face ID / Touch ID, if a parent turned it on).
  • The screens your child uses have no links to other websites and no way for your child to buy anything. When practice is not available, your child only sees “Ask your grown-up”; the app never encourages your child to buy anything.
  • The app does not use notifications, and it is not designed to keep children using it for long periods.

10. Web version

There is also a web version of the app, in Japanese only. Its records are also saved only in your browser, and it has no purchases. This page and the web version are hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc.). When you open a page, GitHub's servers may keep normal access logs (such as IP addresses), but the developer does not receive or use them.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date below. If we make a major change to how information is handled, we will explain it clearly in the app description or on this page.
