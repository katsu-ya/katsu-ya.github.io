Tamago Math: Times Tables (a math practice app for elementary school children)

About the app

  • Practice addition, subtraction, times tables (including the 10s, 11s and 12s) and division, word problems, column math and telling the time.
  • Try it free for 30 days. You won't be charged automatically when the trial ends. To keep using every feature after that, subscribe monthly.
  • No ads. The screens your child uses have no links to other websites and no purchase prompts.
  • Records and names are saved only on your device and are never sent anywhere (Privacy Policy).
  • Works without an internet connection.
  • Available in English and Japanese.

Price, free trial and cancelling

How much does it cost?

The first 30 days are free. To keep practicing after that, subscribe to the auto-renewing monthly subscription. The price for your country is shown in the app (“For Parents” → “Your plan”) and by Apple before you pay. There is no free or paid version: every feature works the same during the trial and while subscribed. There is no yearly plan.

Even before starting the trial, your child can try one round (10 problems) after installing the app.

How do I start the 30-day free trial?
  1. Open “For Parents” (it opens with the parent passcode).
  2. In “Your plan” at the top, tap “Start the 30-day free trial”.
  3. Confirm Apple's purchase sheet (it's free).

The day you tap the button is day 1, and the trial lasts until the end of day 30 (for example, if you start on November 1, it lasts until November 30). “Your plan” shows how many days are left and the end date.

The free trial is only for first-time users. If your Apple ID has subscribed before, the trial button won't appear (“Your plan” says the trial is only for first-time users).

Will I be charged automatically when the trial ends?

No. The trial is a free purchase and does not turn into a subscription by itself. From 3 days before the end, “Your plan” shows a notice and “Your 30-day look back”.

After the trial ends, practice stops, but records (character, badges, calendar and learning report) are kept, and you can still view them and make backups. If you subscribe later, your child can continue where they left off.

How do I subscribe?

Open “For Parents” → “Your plan”, tap “Subscribe”, and confirm with Apple. You can subscribe before or during the trial (your subscription starts that day; if you are on the trial, the remaining trial days end). Payment is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically every month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I cancel?

Go to iPhone/iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose this app and tap “Cancel Subscription”. You can open the same screen from “For Parents” → “Your plan” → “Manage or cancel”.

After cancelling, you can keep using the app until the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple ID you used.

What happens if I reinstall the app or change devices?

The trial and subscription are recorded with your Apple ID. With the same Apple ID, they carry over when you reinstall or use a new device (the trial cannot be restarted). If the plan doesn't show correctly, tap “Restore purchases” in “Your plan”.

Records (name, character, calendar, learning report and so on) are only on the device, so deleting the app deletes them. If you made a file with “💾 Save records” before, load it after reinstalling with “For Parents” → “📂 Load records” to continue where you left off (see “Can I move records to a new device?” below).

Can my family use it on their devices? (Family Sharing)

Yes, the app supports Family Sharing. Members of the purchaser's family group (for example, siblings' or parents' devices) can use it at no extra cost. If it doesn't work on a family member's device, tap “For Parents” → “Restore purchases” on that device.

Can my child buy something by accident?

The purchase and subscription buttons are only inside “For Parents”, which opens with the parent passcode. The screens your child uses never show purchase prompts (when practice isn't available, they only show “Ask your grown-up”).

If “Ask to Buy” is on for your child's Apple ID, the purchase waits for a parent's approval and becomes available once approved.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the parent screen (“For Parents”)?

Tap “For Parents” at the bottom of the start screen. It has your plan (trial and subscription), the learning report, name changes, backups and the language setting.

The first time, you answer a question for grown-ups and then choose a 4-digit parent passcode. The question asks you to type the digits shown in a certain way (for example, from largest to smallest).

How do I switch between English and Japanese?

The app follows your iPhone/iPad language: Japanese if your device is set to Japanese, English otherwise. You can also change it in “For Parents” → “言語 / Language” (Auto / 日本語 / English), or in iPhone/iPad Settings → Apps → Tamago Math → Language.

Records are shared between both languages, so you can switch at any time.

I forgot the parent passcode
  1. On the passcode screen, tap “Forgot passcode”.
  2. Answer the question for grown-ups.
  3. Choose a new passcode. Your child's records stay as they are.
Can I open it with Face ID or Touch ID?

From version 1.3, “For Parents” no longer opens with Face ID or Touch ID (apps for children can't use Face ID or Touch ID to confirm a parent). Please use the 4-digit parent passcode. The device passcode does not open it either.

If you forgot the passcode, follow “I forgot the parent passcode” above to choose a new one. Your child's records stay as they are.

Can I move records to a new device?
  1. On the current device, tap “For Parents” → “Save records”, then “Save to Files” and save it to iCloud Drive or similar.
  2. Install the app on the new device, tap “For Parents” → “Load records”, and choose that file.

The parent passcode belongs to each device, so it doesn't carry over. Please set it again on the new device.

How do I delete records?

Deleting the app from the device deletes all records, names and settings. If you made backup files, please delete them too.

There's no sound

If your iPhone/iPad is in Silent mode, sound effects and read aloud may not be heard. Turn off Silent mode and turn up the volume. Also check that “Sound effects” and “Read aloud” are on in “⚙️ Settings” in the app.

Can I change the reading voice or make it sound more natural?

Choose a voice in “⚙️ Settings” → “Reading voice”. On iPhone/iPad, you can download higher-quality voices in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English.
